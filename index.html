How it works

Simple steps.
Zero courage required.

Enter the email of the person you want to Veil. Works with any email including .edu. They receive a secure link. A 24-hour chat opens when they respond. No pressure. No record. You can Veil the same person once every 48 hours. Your code name changes every time.
01

Enter your email

No profile. No username. No photo. Just your email so the right Veil finds you.

02

Send a Veil

Enter their email. Add a hint or stay fully anonymous. They receive a secure link. One Veil per person every 48 hours.

03

You get a code name

The moment you send, you are assigned a random code name. Something like Velvet Raccoon or Midnight Waffle. Changes every time.

04

They check their Veil

They open the link and see a Veil waiting with your code name. They can choose to Veil back or let it sit.

05

24-hour chat opens

When they Veil back, a private chat opens immediately. The 24-hour timer starts. After 24 hours the chat deletes itself completely.

06

Lift the Veil

Inside the chat, either person can press "Lift the Veil." Both must press it within the 24-hour window to reveal each other's email.

The idea

The Poke was ambiguous by design. Zuckerberg himself described it that way. It became one of the most iconic features of early social media not because of what it did, but because of what it implied. A way to say something without saying anything.

But that was 2004. Today every platform asks you to perform. To post, to follow, to be visible. The quiet signal got lost.

Veil is that signal back. Secure. Anonymous. And it actually leads somewhere real. You should not be afraid of rejection because nothing is ever attached to your name unless you want it to be.
